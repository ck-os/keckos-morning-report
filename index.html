Crude Spike and Iran Rhetoric Set the Tone as Risk Comes Off

Futures slide, the VIX jumps, and Treasury yields give back Wednesday's decline as oil leads the tape.
Markets Desk · Premarket

Premarket futures are lower across the board -- S&P 500 futures (ES=F) down 0.65% to 7,688.25, Nasdaq futures (NQ=F) off a sharper 1.13% to 29,279.25, Dow futures (YM=F) down 0.80%, and Russell futures (RTY=F) down 0.96% -- as WTI crude (CL=F) jumps 3.67% to $87.59 on President Trump's sharpened rhetoric toward Iran amid a stalled negotiations process. The VIX is up 7.32% to 15.98, still a low absolute level but a notable one-morning jump, and the 10-year Treasury yield (^TNX) is up 1.14% to 4.71% as it rebounds from Wednesday's sharp pullback tied to Treasury Secretary Bessent's debt-buyback intervention. Gold (GC=F) is down 0.91% to $4,514.10 even with the risk-off tone, while the dollar index (DX-Y.NYB) is roughly flat at 98.77 and bitcoin (BTC-USD) is bucking the broader risk-off move, up 3.50% to $71,728 on Trump's push for Congress to pass the crypto Clarity Act.

Oil is doing the talking this morning -- a 3.67% jump on Iran rhetoric is pulling equity futures and the VIX along with it, while bitcoin and yields are each moving on their own separate story.

Overseas, the picture is split along east-west lines: Asia outperformed overnight, with the Nikkei 225 up 1.2% to 66,216.79 and the Hang Seng up 0.8% to 25,698.49, while Europe is softer, with Germany's DAX down 0.76% and the UK's FTSE 100 down 0.53%. Crude's move is the clearest cross-asset thread tying today's session together -- it's pressuring equity futures and lifting the VIX at the same time bond yields are separately normalizing after Wednesday's Treasury-driven dip, a reminder that geopolitical and rates-driven forces are working on the tape independently this morning rather than from a single cause.
