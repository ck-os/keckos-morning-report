Rate Hike Digested, Volatility Unwinds Across the Board

A quarter-point Fed hike lands as a relief rather than a shock, with the VIX's near-double-digit drop the clearest tell.
Markets Desk · Premarket

The Federal Reserve unanimously raised its benchmark rate a quarter point on Wednesday, according to CNBC, a move Chair Kevin Warsh delivered despite repeated public pressure from President Trump for lower rates -- and rather than unsettling markets, it appears to have removed a source of overhang. S&P futures are up 0.80% to 7,684.00 and Nasdaq futures lead at +1.04% to 29,561.00 this morning, but the more telling number is the VIX, down 9.49% to 16.03 -- a scale of volatility unwind consistent with positioning that had been braced for a harder outcome than what actually arrived. That relief is showing up beyond equities: crude is down 1.25% to $101.15 and gold has slipped 0.90% to $4,347.90, both consistent with reduced hedging demand rather than any fresh flight-to-safety trade, while the 10-year yield firmed slightly to 5.01% as the market recalibrates to a marginally higher rate path.

The VIX's near-double-digit drop is the clearest tell that Wednesday's hike removed uncertainty rather than added to it.

Beyond the Fed reaction itself, the tape is also digesting a wave of AI-infrastructure dealmaking that touches several of the same mega-cap names: CNBC reports Salesforce raised its fiscal 2030 revenue target to over $63 billion at its Dreamforce conference, Snap is partnering with Nvidia (NVDA, $217.23, +1.56% premarket), AWS and Salesforce to push its AR glasses into the enterprise market, and Arm's CEO said he's more confident in hitting a $2 billion AI-chip revenue goal -- a cluster of announcements that helps explain why chip and platform names are among today's premarket gainers even without a single dominant earnings catalyst. There's no scheduled US economic data release today per the calendar (the next is PCE and GDP on September 30), so the rate decision itself is the dominant event driving positioning into the open.
