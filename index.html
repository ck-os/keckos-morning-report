Nvidia's Guidance Beat Lifts the AI Trade, But the Bond and Gold Markets Aren't Fully On Board

A blockbuster chip earnings print is broadening across AI-adjacent names, while yields and gold move in a way that complicates a simple risk-on read.
Markets Desk · Premarket

Nvidia shares are up roughly 7% premarket after CNBC reported the chip giant's revenue guidance reassured investors that AI demand will remain strong -- a reversal from Wednesday's session, when Investor's Business Daily noted Nvidia had actually lost ground into the print while the Dow slid on inflation data. The relief is spreading well past the one ticker: Stocktwits flagged Micron, Marvell, Okta, Salesforce, and Rocket Lab as names in focus alongside Nvidia this morning, a genuine AI-infrastructure story rather than an isolated single-stock pop. That concentration shows up cleanly in the futures: Nasdaq futures are the only one of the four major contracts in positive territory (+0.16%), while Dow futures lag at -0.42% and S&P futures are roughly flat at -0.06% -- exactly the pattern you'd expect when the catalyst sits in the tech-heavy index's biggest weighting and doesn't lift cyclicals or small caps the same way.

Beneath the Nvidia-led bounce, the cross-asset signature is more mixed than a clean risk-on morning would suggest: the 10-year yield is up about half a percent to 4.66% and gold is down roughly 1% to $4,631.60, a combination that doesn't line up with equities moving higher on good news alone. That tension likely traces back to Wednesday's inflation data, which IBD reported drove the Dow lower into the close -- Nvidia's guidance appears to be resetting the AI-earnings narrative this morning without fully resetting the rates story underneath it. There's no US economic release scheduled today (the next major prints -- the September jobs report, PPI, and CPI -- don't land until early-to-mid September), so today's session is left to trade off the earnings reaction and whatever follow-through develops in yields as the cash open approaches.

Nvidia's Guidance Headlines a Broad AI-Earnings Morning

Markets Desk
Nvidia (NVDA) --

Shares are up roughly 7% premarket after CNBC reported the company's revenue guidance reassured investors that AI demand will remain strong, a sharp reversal from Wednesday's session when the stock had lost ground heading into the print.
