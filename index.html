Marvell's Guidance Miss Weighs on Chips as Market Awaits Warsh

A single earnings-driven chip selloff is dragging the Nasdaq below its peers, layered on top of defensive positioning ahead of a scheduled Fed speech.
Markets Desk · Premarket

Marvell Technology shares are down 8% in premarket trading despite reporting 37% revenue growth, after the company's fiscal 2028 outlook fell short of what investors wanted to see -- and the reaction isn't isolated to Marvell alone. Stocktwits flagged Nvidia, Micron, IREN, PayPal, and Rocket Lab all trading in sympathy this morning as the same chip/AI-infrastructure story pulls the broader group lower, even as retail sentiment on Stocktwits reportedly stayed "bullish" on the S&P-tracking SPY. That concentration shows up cleanly in the index math: Nasdaq futures are down 0.24%, the weakest of the four major futures contracts, while Dow futures are actually positive at 0.12% and S&P futures are roughly flat at 0.03% -- a gap that lines up with the Nasdaq's much heavier weighting in the semiconductor and AI-infrastructure names caught up in Marvell's miss. Layered on top of that single-stock story is a second, separate dynamic: new Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh delivers his first Jackson Hole speech today, and gold's 0.76% rise to $4,644.60 alongside a 10-year yield that's ticked up to 4.69% looks like cautious positioning ahead of that address rather than a reaction to any surprise.

Marvell Slides on Guidance, Salesforce Rides Its Second-Best Day Ever

One chip name disappoints on outlook, one software name delivers on AI, and Imax finds itself with more interest than offers.
Markets Desk
Marvell Technology (MRVL) --

Shares are down 8% in premarket trading even after a second-quarter revenue beat with 37% growth, as the company's fiscal 2028 outlook underwhelmed Wall Street. The stock's slide is dragging on other chip and AI-infrastructure names named alongside it this morning, including Nvidia, Micron, and IREN, per Stocktwits -- a reminder that guidance, not the trailing quarter, is what's setting the tone for AI-adjacent semiconductor names right now.

Salesforce (CRM) --

CNBC reports Salesforce's stock notched its second-best trading day on record following its latest earnings, as CEO Marc Benioff's AI growth story wins back skeptical investors. The report frames it as a genuine sentiment shift on Salesforce's AI strategy specifically, not a broader software-sector move.

Imax (IMAX) --

CNBC reports Imax has signaled openness to a sale even as its stock and box-office results are setting records, but says studio conflicts and a rich valuation are complicating the field of potential bidders -- a story about deal friction rather than a premarket price move.
