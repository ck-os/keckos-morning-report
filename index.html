Company Briefs

Moderna Surges on Cancer Vaccine Data, Target Raises Outlook

Two distinct company stories are moving premarket trading on real news: a biotech breakthrough and a retail earnings beat.
Markets Desk
Moderna (MRNA) --

Moderna shares are up roughly 50% premarket after a late-stage trial of its combination cancer vaccine with Merck's Keytruda showed the regimen significantly extended the time melanoma patients lived without recurrence, compared with Keytruda alone. This is a clinical-trial catalyst specific to the company, distinct from the broader biotech sector's day-to-day moves, and marks one of the more significant single-stock premarket moves in recent memory for the name.

Target (TGT) --

Target raised its full-year outlook after posting better-than-expected second-quarter sales, with the company citing a rebound in sales alongside a sizable tariff refund that boosted the bottom line. The tariff-refund detail is a one-time item worth distinguishing from the underlying sales rebound when assessing how much of the beat is repeatable.

Amazon (AMZN) --

Amazon is expanding its drone delivery service to nearly 500 cities, following a regulatory approval enabling longer-range flights, with an internal target of 1 million deliveries this year. This is an operational/logistics expansion story rather than an earnings or guidance event, and isn't showing up as a premarket price mover on its own in today's data.
