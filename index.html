Crude oil is doing virtually all of the morning's real work: WTI is down 5.78% to $90.24 after CNBC reported that Saudi Arabia aims to restart a critical pipeline this week, a headline that reversed early gains and compounds improving diplomatic signals between the U.S. and Iran over the Strait of Hormuz.
