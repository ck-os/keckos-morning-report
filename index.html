Iran's Hormuz Overture Sends Oil Down 6%, Cooling Monday's AI Rally

A diplomatic signal out of the UN General Assembly is doing more to the tape than yesterday's AI-fueled Nasdaq surge.
Markets Desk · Premarket

Crude is the story this morning: WTI has dropped 6.3% to $89.78 a barrel after Iran reportedly told diplomats at the UN General Assembly that it could reopen the Strait of Hormuz within seven days if the U.S. eases military pressure, per CNBC. That's a meaningful de-escalation signal for a market that's been pricing a war premium into oil since the U.S.-Iran conflict flared, and it's landing the same morning Treasury Secretary Bessent and other officials are said to be pursuing the diplomatic track further at the UN. Equity futures, by contrast, are barely moving -- S&P futures +0.04%, Nasdaq futures +0.05% -- a muted follow-through to Monday's session, when the Nasdaq closed nearly 3% higher and AMD (AMD, $611.99, -0.57% premarket) crossed a $1 trillion market cap on AI-infrastructure enthusiasm that also lifted Arm, Meta, and Amazon. The 10-year yield easing 0.7% and the VIX slipping to 14.84 suggest no fresh anxiety is being priced in behind the oil move -- this reads as risk being taken OFF the crude trade specifically, not a broader flight from risk.

Crude's 6% drop on an Iran diplomacy signal is doing more work on the tape this morning than yesterday's AI rally is getting credit for in premarket futures.

Small caps are the standout mover in today's Glance basket, with Russell 2000 futures up 0.42% versus a nearly flat S&P and Nasdaq -- a rotation that lines up with the calmer VIX (14.84) and easing 10-year yield (4.96%, -0.7%), both of which tend to favor smaller, more rate-sensitive names when pressure comes off. There's no scheduled U.S. economic release today to tie that move to -- the next data points on the calendar are PCE and GDP a week out (September 30) and the jobs report on October 2 -- so today's moves look driven by the Iran headline and digestion of Monday's AI rally rather than any data catalyst.
