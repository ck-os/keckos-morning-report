Nvidia's Guidance Beat Lifts the Whole Chip Complex, Nasdaq Outpaces the Dow

A single earnings print is doing the work of a market-wide story this morning, with the cross-asset tape only partly confirming a clean risk-on read.
Markets Desk · Premarket

Nvidia shares jumped about 7% premarket after CNBC reported the chip giant's quarterly revenue guidance reassured investors that AI demand will remain strong -- and the reaction isn't staying contained to one ticker. A Stocktwits roundup this morning named Micron, Marvell, and Okta, alongside Salesforce and Rocket Lab, as stocks "in focus" on the same premarket move, evidence that the market is treating this as a read-through for the broader AI-infrastructure trade rather than an isolated single-stock print. That concentration shows up cleanly in the futures basket: Nasdaq futures are up 0.19% while Dow futures are down 0.39% and Russell futures down 0.17% -- a gap directly explained by the Nasdaq's much heavier weighting in the semiconductor and software names swept up in the Nvidia story. The VIX easing to 14.93 (-1.84%) is consistent with that read, but it's not a clean confirmation across every asset: gold is down nearly 1% and the 10-year yield is up to 4.66%, a combination more typical of risk appetite pulling money out of havens than of a textbook flight-to-safety or flight-from-safety signature either way.

Nvidia and CrowdStrike Headline a Morning of AI-Driven Earnings Reactions

Two chip- and security-adjacent names are moving on genuine earnings surprises, with the reaction spilling into peer tickers.
Markets Desk
Nvidia (NVDA) --

Shares rose roughly 7% premarket after the company's quarterly revenue guidance reassured investors that AI demand will remain strong, per CNBC. The reaction is spreading well beyond the stock itself: a Stocktwits premarket roundup named Micron (MU), Marvell (MRVL), and Okta (OKTA) among the names "in focus" on the same story, and IBD's Wednesday close coverage had already flagged Micron and Sandisk climbing into the print. Retail sentiment on Stocktwits reportedly improved to "neutral" on SPY, though it remained "bearish" on QQQ heading into the report.

CrowdStrike (CRWD) --

Shares jumped 11% Wednesday after a record second quarter, which CNBC's coverage attributed to what the company is calling a "Mythos moment" -- rising demand for advanced security tools as frontier AI expands the threat surface. The stock is now up 61% year-to-date on the back of the print, one of the clearer standalone earnings winners heading into today's session.
