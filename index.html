Nvidia's Guidance Beat Powers a Nasdaq-Led Premarket Rally

A single earnings print is doing the work across an entire AI-adjacent basket, while the rest of the tape stays comparatively muted.
Markets Desk · Premarket

Nvidia shares are up roughly 7% premarket after CNBC reported the chip giant's second-quarter results and forward revenue guidance reassured investors that AI infrastructure demand isn't slowing. The move isn't staying contained to one ticker: Stocktwits flagged Micron, Marvell, Okta, Salesforce, and RocketLab all trading actively on the same catalyst this morning, and CrowdStrike is carrying its own momentum into the session after jumping 11% Wednesday on a record quarter it credited to AI-driven cybersecurity demand. That concentration is showing up cleanly in the futures spread: Nasdaq futures are up 1.05% versus 0.42% for the S&P 500 and a roughly flat Dow (-0.08%) and Russell 2000 (-0.07%), exactly the pattern you'd expect when the story is centered on chips and AI-software names that carry far more weight in the Nasdaq than in the other three.

Nasdaq futures are up 1.05% versus 0.42% for the S&P 500 and a roughly flat Dow -- the gap a direct consequence of how much AI-chip and software exposure sits in one index and not the others.

The cross-asset tape is largely confirming that this is a sector story, not a broad risk-on move: the VIX has eased to 14.97 (-1.58%), consistent with the rally, but gold is also up 0.87% to $4,638 and the 10-year yield has ticked higher to 4.66% -- a combination that doesn't fit a clean flight-to-safety or flight-to-risk read and instead looks like money rotating into a specific trade rather than a market-wide shift in sentiment. There's no major US economic release scheduled today (the next dated items -- the jobs report and PPI -- don't land until September), so barring fresh headlines, Nvidia's print and its ripple through the AI-adjacent names look like the primary driver into the open.
