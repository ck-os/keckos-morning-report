Crude Climbs on Iran Rhetoric, Yields Ease Back

Futures are modestly lower across the board as oil jumps and Treasury yields pull back from Wednesday's highs.
Markets Desk · Premarket

Equity futures are pointing to a soft open, with the S&P 500 (ES=F) down 0.16% to 7,726.00, the Nasdaq (NQ=F) off 0.38% to 29,500.00, the Dow (YM=F) down 0.24% to 53,427, and small caps (RTY=F) lower by 0.26% to 3,038.30. The standout move is in crude oil, with WTI (CL=F) up 2.79% to $86.85 as the Trump administration escalates pressure on Iran, threatening an "Economic D-Day" and further sanctions on its backers. The 10-year Treasury yield (^TNX) is easing to 4.65%, down 1.13%, as traders digest a Treasury debt buyback plan a day after a rally in long bonds. The VIX is up modestly to 15.11, still a low reading overall.

Crude is up nearly 3% on renewed Iran pressure even as yields ease and equity futures drift lower in a narrow band.

Overseas, Asian markets outperformed while Europe lagged: the Nikkei 225 gained 1.2% to 66,216.79 and the Hang Seng added 0.8% to 25,698.49, while Germany's DAX slipped 0.52% to 25,991.16 and London's FTSE 100 fell 0.35% to 10,709.71. Gold (GC=F) is down 0.23% to $4,545.00 and the dollar index (DX-Y.NYB) is off 0.12% to 98.69, a fairly quiet cross-asset picture outside of oil and crypto. Bitcoin (BTC-USD) is up 3.80% to $71,933 after President Trump urged Congress to pass the crypto Clarity Act, a regulatory framework bill for the sector -- a notable move given the dollar and gold aren't showing a comparable risk-on or risk-off signal today.

Alibaba (BABA) --

U.S.-listed shares fell roughly 3% in premarket trading after the company reported a 75% drop in net income for the June quarter, which it attributed to heavy AI-related spending. Ahead of its fiscal second-quarter earnings report due today, Walmart said it is raising its full-year outlook and will use a large tariff refund to help keep prices low for consumers. The report is expected to offer an early read on consumer health and the K-shaped economy this morning.
