Oil Jumps on Iran Tension, Crypto Rallies, Futures Little Changed

Crude and bitcoin lead a mixed premarket tape while equity futures drift modestly lower.
Markets Desk · Premarket

Equity futures are pointing to a soft open, with S&P 500 futures down 0.14% to 7,728.25, Nasdaq futures off 0.19%, and small-cap Russell futures the weakest of the group at -0.27%. The more notable action is in commodities and crypto: WTI crude is up 2.60% to $86.69 after President Trump threatened an "Economic D-Day" for Iran, reviving a geopolitical risk premium, while bitcoin has surged 4.02% to $72,088 on Trump's push for Congress to pass the crypto Clarity Act.
