10-Year Yield Hits a 2002 High, Futures Slip, VIX Jumps

The 10-year yield pushing to its highest since 2002 is the morning's real story, with oil's bounce back above $90 adding to the inflation worry ahead of today's Fed minutes and 10-year auction. VIX has jumped 5.46% to 15.83 but that's a normal pre-auction jitter, not a stress signal -- it's still inside its 6-month range and well below the 25.78 high.

Top Story 10-Year Yield Hits Highest Since 2002 The 10-year sits at 5.35%, up again this morning; Technology led Tuesday's gains but is the sector most exposed to higher discount rates on long-duration growth earnings -- futures for the Nasdaq (-0.77%) are underperforming the Dow (-0.83%) is actually similar here, so this is a broad, cross-asset reaction rather than a tech-only story.
Also Moving Oil Rebounds as Diesel Shortage, Iran Tensions Persist WTI crude is up 0.92% to $90.26 as Rubio says Iran missed 'multiple' chances for a nuclear deal and heating oil costs are projected to jump 21% this winter -- Energy is a small slice of the S&P, so this shows up more in household heating bills and diesel-dependent sectors than in the index itself.
Worth Knowing European Bank Shares Fall as Yields Surge, Spreads Widen Not a US story yet, but it's the same mechanism playing out abroad -- widening credit spreads alongside the yield spike is exactly the kind of cross-market confirmation worth watching if it starts showing up in US financials too.
