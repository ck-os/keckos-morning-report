Oil Extends Its Spike as Iran Tension Lingers and Volatility Stays Awake

The Strait of Hormuz scare from earlier this morning hasn't faded -- WTI crude has pushed further to $95.89 (+3.07%), and the VIX has climbed to 16.51 (+5.03%), both moving in the same direction as gold's push to $4,437 (+0.99%), a combination that reads as a genuine, broadening risk-off tone rather than an isolated oil story. Equity futures are lower across the board (S&P futures -0.43%, Nasdaq futures -0.63%, Dow futures -0.74%), with the healthcare and financial sectors leading declines in the underlying cash market, while Meta (META, $644.00, +4.97% premarket) remains the one standalone name cutting against the defensive grain.

The Standout WTI crude has extended its overnight spike to +3.07% at $95.89, still tracking the unresolved Iran-U.S. tension near the Strait of Hormuz first reported earlier this morning.
The Outlier Meta (META, $644.00, +4.97% premarket) remains the lone standalone gainer of size, cutting sharply against an otherwise risk-off tape.
The Rest of the Tape Equity futures, a firmer VIX (16.51, +5.03%), and firmer gold ($4,437.40, +0.99%) are all leaning the same defensive direction, while European indices (DAX -1.7%, FTSE -1.09%) are down more sharply than Asia overnight.
CBOE Volatility Index (VIX), 1-month. The VIX's jump to 16.51 (+5.03%) tracks the deepening Iran-U.S. tension story and the broader risk-off cross-asset move described above.
Gold rising alongside a firmer VIX and weaker equity futures is a textbook flight-to-safety signature, not just stocks drifting on their own.

Inside the cash market, the sector tape shows exactly where the pressure is landing: XLV (healthcare) is down 2.52% today, XLF (financials) down 1.38%, and broader breadth confirms the weakness is unusually narrow-based -- the equal-weight S&P proxy (RSP) is down 1.04% against the cap-weighted SPY's 0.55% decline, meaning the mega-caps are cushioning the index-level damage while the average stock is faring notably worse. The one clear exception is Meta (META, $644.00, +4.97% premarket), still holding a standalone gain even as its megacap software peers Alphabet (GOOGL, $331.49, -2.03%) and Amazon (AMZN, $252.91, -1.58%) trade lower alongside the broader tape. No major U.S. economic release lands today -- PPI is scheduled for tomorrow and CPI for Friday -- so today's session is left to trade primarily on the oil/geopolitical headline flow rather than a scheduled data catalyst.
