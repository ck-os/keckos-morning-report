Crude Climbs on Iran Rhetoric as Equity Futures Drift

Oil is the standout mover premarket while stocks, gold, and the dollar all soften modestly and yields ease back.

Equity futures are pointing to a soft open, with S&P 500 futures (ES=F) down 0.15% to 7,727.00, Nasdaq futures (NQ=F) off 0.22% to 29,548.00, Dow futures (YM=F) down 0.20% to 53,450, and small-cap Russell futures (RTY=F) lagging at -0.29% to 3,037.20. The VIX is up 1.81% to 15.16 -- still a low absolute level, but a tick higher rather than lower. The real action is in commodities and crypto: WTI crude (CL=F) is up 2.49% to $86.59 as President Trump escalates rhetoric toward Iran, threatening an 'Economic D-Day' and further consequences for Tehran's backers, while bitcoin (BTC-USD) has surged 3.58% to $71,784 after Trump urged Congress to pass the crypto Clarity Act. Gold (GC=F) is down slightly at $4,543.20 (-0.27%), the dollar index (DX-Y.NYB) is off 0.21% to 98.61, and the 10-year yield (^TNX) has eased 1.13% to 4.65%, a partial continuation of Wednesday's yield pullback tied to the Treasury's debt buyback plan.

Oil's Iran-driven jump and bitcoin's policy-driven surge are pulling in opposite directions from a broader tape that's otherwise drifting lower.

Overseas, Asia outperformed Europe overnight: the Nikkei 225 gained 1.2% to 66,216.79 and Hong Kong's Hang Seng rose 0.8% to 25,698.49, while Germany's DAX slipped 0.54% to 25,986.10 and London's FTSE 100 eased 0.22% to 10,723.73. Treasury market attention remains on the fiscal backdrop -- U.S. government debt has now passed $40 trillion, more than doubling over the past decade -- alongside continued public pressure from Trump on the Fed to cut rates further, a dynamic worth watching against today's yield move rather than treating as settled.
