Top Story Core PCE Cools to 3.0%, Under the 3.7%/3.3% Forecast Technology is 38.7% of the S&P and is contributing +0.193 pts today (40% of the index's move) -- the biggest single beneficiary of a cooler inflation read easing pressure off yields.
