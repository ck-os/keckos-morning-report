MARKETS · MARKETS DESK

Futures Lower, Yields Rise, Volatility Wakes Up

Small caps lead a broad premarket pullback in equity futures as the 10-year yield climbs and the VIX pops, even as oil, gold and bitcoin all trade higher.

Equity futures are pointing to a weaker open across the board Thursday: S&P 500 futures (ES=F) are off 0.64% to 7,689.25, Nasdaq futures (NQ=F) down 1.00% to 29,315.75, Dow futures (YM=F) down 0.96% to 53,041, and Russell 2000 futures (RTY=F) the laggard at -1.57% to 2,998.30. The VIX is up sharply, 6.11% to 15.80, while the 10-year Treasury yield (^TNX) has ticked up to 4.70%, a 0.97% move that keeps pressure on rate-sensitive corners of the market. Treasury Secretary Bessent said the department's buyback operation targeting longer-dated securities, where yields have been surging, could exceed $4 billion.

Small caps are leading the pullback while oil, gold and bitcoin all trade higher -- not a clean risk-off morning.

The cross-asset picture is more mixed than the equity futures alone suggest: crude oil (CL=F) is up 2.25% to $86.39, gold (GC=F) is up a modest 0.29% to $4,568.80, the dollar index (DX-Y.NYB) is roughly flat at 98.92, and bitcoin (BTC-USD) has jumped 5.01% to $72,774 -- CNBC reports bitcoin is up 12% over two days on a last-ditch push by President Trump and crypto executives for the Clarity Act. Overseas, Asian markets outperformed European ones overnight: the Nikkei 225 closed up 1.2% and the Hang Seng up 0.8%, while the DAX slipped 0.42% and the FTSE was essentially flat at +0.04%.

Walmart Slips Despite Raised Guidance

A retail bellwether's earnings reaction is the clearest single-stock story on an otherwise macro-heavy morning.

Walmart (WMT) --

Walmart raised its full-year outlook and disclosed a large tariff refund alongside fiscal second-quarter results, but shares fell on underlying sales concerns, per CNBC. The report is being read as a broader signal on the health of the consumer and the so-called K-shaped economy, where higher-income and lower-income spending patterns are diverging.
