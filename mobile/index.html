Markets Brace for a Rate Hike, Not a Cut, as Fed Decision Lands Today

The Fed's decision lands this afternoon, and for once the widely-priced outcome is a hike, not a cut -- CNBC reports traders see a 92% chance of a rate increase, which is why the 10-year yield is hovering above 5% and why the S&P, Nasdaq, and Dow all closed lower yesterday as investors priced it in. Futures are modestly higher into the decision (Nasdaq futures +0.42%, S&P futures +0.20%) alongside a small VIX pullback to 16.98, but that calm sits next to a $104 crude price still elevated on Iran-conflict supply fears and a 10-year yield at 5.00% -- both signs the underlying tension hasn't actually resolved, it's just paused ahead of the announcement. Retail sales also print today alongside the Fed decision, adding a second data point the market could react to before the close.

With the FOMC decision and retail sales both landing today, expect the bulk of today's real move to come after both are in hand, not from this premarket drift.
