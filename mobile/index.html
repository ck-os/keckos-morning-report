MARKETS · MARKETS DESK

Futures edge higher as bitcoin roars and yields grind up

A quiet risk-on tone across equities and commodities sits alongside a genuine bond-market undercurrent worth watching.

S&P 500 futures are up 0.23% to 7,686.75 in premarket trade, with the Nasdaq (+0.42% to 29,484.00), Dow (+0.26% to 53,015) and Russell 2000 (+0.32% to 3,018.20) all firmer alongside them. The VIX has eased 2.06% to 15.68, ordinarily a signal of calm, but it's sitting next to a 10-year Treasury yield up 0.92% to 4.70% -- CNBC ties the yield move to Treasury Secretary Bessent's bond-buyback program losing steam, with JPMorgan's James Sullivan likening the broader intervention effort to "paying your mortgage with your credit card." Commodities are joining the firmer tone, with WTI crude up 0.91% to $87.25 and gold up 0.99% to $4,636.50, while the dollar index is essentially flat at 98.64.

Bitcoin's near-7% overnight surge and a 20% weekly gain stand out even against an otherwise calm-looking tape.

Overseas, the picture is mixed: Hong Kong's Hang Seng is up 1.21%, Germany's DAX is fractionally higher at +0.1%, while Japan's Nikkei 225 is down 0.22% and London's FTSE is off 0.07%. Bitcoin is the clearest standout of the morning, up 6.79% to roughly $77,973 and on pace for a 20% weekly gain per CNBC, alongside a Goldman Sachs note flagging hedge funds' worst July underperformance versus the S&P 500 in more than two decades of data -- a reminder that positioning, not just headlines, is part of this tape. Geopolitical threads (Romania scrambling jets near a European gas project, and Vance's comments on economic pressure toward Iran) are present in the news flow but not yet visibly moving futures or yields this morning.
