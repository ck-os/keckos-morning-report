THIS MORNING · Premarket

Iran's Hormuz Offer Holds, Oil Extends Slide as VIX Nears 6-Month Low

Iran's offer to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within seven days and restart nuclear talks is still the tape's dominant story this morning, and the de-escalation trade has deepened rather than faded since the last check: WTI crude is now down 1.90% to $92.81 and VIX has slid to 15.12, within a point of its 6-month low of 14.25. Futures are broadly higher (S&P +0.29%, Nasdaq +0.49%, Dow +0.29%), but gold's continued 0.96% gain to $4,339 shows the safe-haven bid hasn't fully unwound -- likely tied to lingering unease over Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's hawkish policy signals, reported overnight to be meeting internal resistance even as he pushes ahead. This reads as a genuine but partial risk-on reaction, not an all-clear.
