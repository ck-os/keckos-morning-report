Oil Spike and Fed Minutes Push Yields Higher, Stocks Pull Back

Oil's 5% jump to $92.72 on Iran-linked shipping disruptions is landing on top of Wednesday's Fed minutes flagging another hike this year -- together pushing the 10-year yield to 5.27%, and that combination is hitting Tech hardest: the sector is -0.89% and responsible for 62% of the S&P's -0.58% premarket slide.

Top Story Fed Minutes Flag Another Hike, Yields Climb Further Tech is 40.5% of the S&P and down 0.89%, explaining 62% of the index's -0.58% move -- long-duration names are the first to feel a 5.27% 10-year.
Also Moving Oil Jumps 5% as Iran Conflict Disrupts Shipping Routes Energy is only 3.4% of the S&P, so its 1.72% gain adds just 0.058 pts back -- nowhere near enough to offset Tech's drag.
Worth Knowing PepsiCo Cuts Guidance on Slow North America Turnaround Not in the index math yet -- PEP itself is actually up 2.34% in Staples premarket, so the cut is being read as already-priced rather than a fresh shock.
