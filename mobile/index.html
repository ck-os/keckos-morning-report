MARKETS · MARKETS DESK

Risk-Off Widens as Walmart Weighs on Retail, Crude Climbs, Bitcoin Bucks the Trend

Equity futures have extended their premarket losses, yields have fully reversed Wednesday's dip, and crude's Iran-driven spike continues -- while bitcoin's short-squeeze rally stands apart from the broader tape.

The tone into late-morning premarket is firmly risk-off. S&P futures (ES=F) are down 0.50% to 7,700.00, Nasdaq futures (NQ=F) off 0.80% to 29,376.25, Dow futures (YM=F) down 0.83% to 53,109, and small-cap Russell futures (RTY=F) leading declines at -1.27% to 3,007.50. The VIX has jumped 7.45% to 16.00, and the 10-year Treasury yield is up 1.01% to 4.70% -- fully erasing Wednesday's pullback that had followed Treasury Secretary Bessent's intervention. Crude oil (CL=F) is up 2.41% to $86.53 on continued Iran-related headline risk, while gold (GC=F) has eased 0.27% to $4,543.40 and the dollar index (DX-Y.NYB) is roughly flat at 98.87. Bitcoin is the clear outlier, up 3.21% to $71,524 on what's being described as a $3.3 billion short-covering squeeze tied to renewed momentum behind the Clarity Act.

The VIX's jump to 16.00 and a fully-reversed yield dip both point to the same thing: Wednesday's calm didn't last into Thursday.

Overseas, the picture is split. Asian markets closed higher, with the Nikkei 225 up 1.2% to 66,216.79 and the Hang Seng up 0.8% to 25,698.49, while European markets are softer -- the DAX down 0.67% to 25,952.10 and the FTSE 100 off 0.18% to 10,727.96, likely reflecting some spillover from the same rate and oil pressures weighing on U.S. futures. Domestically, Walmart's earnings miss is doing outsized work on sentiment given its read on the consumer, and the crude move is large enough on its own to cross this report's chart threshold -- both are detailed further in Company Briefs and the accompanying chart below.

COMPANY BRIEFS · MARKETS DESK

Company Briefs

Walmart's consumer read dominates, Alibaba's AI spending weighs on its results, and crypto-linked equities rally against the broader tape.

Walmart (WMT) --

Shares are down roughly 8% premarket after the retailer posted its slowest same-store sales growth since 2020, even as it raised its full-year outlook and cited a large tariff refund. The stock's reaction contrasts with Target, which is holding steady, and Costco, which is easing only modestly -- a gap that points to company-specific execution concerns at Walmart rather than a broad retail read-through, per 24/7 Wall St.'s coverage of the divergence.

Alibaba (BABA) --

U.S.-listed shares fell about 5% premarket after the company reported a 75% drop in net income for the June quarter, which CNBC ties to elevated AI infrastructure spending pressuring near-term profitability even as cloud revenue was in focus.

Strategy (MSTR), Bitmine, Coinbase (COIN) --

Crypto-linked equities are rallying against the broader risk-off tape -- Strategy up roughly 9%, Bitmine up about 7%, and Coinbase up around 6% -- as a reported $3.3 billion short-covering squeeze helped push bitcoin back above $71,000, alongside a renewed push by crypto executives and the administration for passage of the Clarity Act.
