Yields Push to Multi-Year Highs Ahead of PPI, Tech Leads Futures Lower

Treasury yields are climbing to multi-year highs this morning as traders position ahead of today's Producer Price Index release, with the 10-year at 4.84% (+0.65%) and CNBC reporting oil above $100 as a contributing pressure on the rate path. Nasdaq futures are the weakest of the four major contracts, down 0.50% versus a roughly flat Dow, as the same higher-yield backdrop that's been weighing on richly-valued tech names continues -- Microsoft, Nvidia, and Apple are all drawing fresh scrutiny in this morning's coverage over whether current prices already reflect the good news. The VIX ticking up to 16.68 and crude's push to $97.51 both point the same direction as the equity softness: a cautious, rates-led tape into this morning's inflation data.

The 10-year's push to 4.84% is landing hardest where it always does first -- the most richly-valued corner of the tape.

Oil is the other number doing real work today: WTI crude is up 1.52% to $97.51, and CNBC's own reporting ties that climb directly to the same rising-yield story, framing crude above the psychologically important $100 level as adding to the case for continued rate pressure. The VIX is up 1.34% to 16.68 -- a modest but confirming move alongside softer equities and higher yields, consistent with a cautious rather than panicked tone into today's data. With PPI due later this morning and CPI following Friday, the next several hours carry real potential to move the tape further depending on how wholesale inflation prints relative to expectations.
