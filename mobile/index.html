Fed Reaction Extends: Yields and Oil Fall, VIX Sinks 12.8%

Markets are still digesting Wednesday's Fed rate decision, with the S&P 500 and Nasdaq notching their best day in six weeks as falling oil prices eased inflation worries and Treasury yields dropped alongside them. The VIX collapsed 12.8% to 15.44 and the 10-year yield eased to 4.95%, both consistent with a market that got the reassurance it wanted from the Fed and from crude's retreat to $101.07. Overnight futures are only marginally softer (S&P futures -0.09%, Nasdaq futures -0.16%), suggesting the bulk of yesterday's relief rally has already been priced rather than reversing.

The Outlier Crude oil's 1.33% drop to $101.07 is being credited directly by wire coverage as the reason the S&P 500 and Nasdaq posted their best session in six weeks, easing inflation concerns just as the Fed's own rate move is being digested.
