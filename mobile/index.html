Top Story Oil Eases on Iran Talks, Yields Follow, Tech Leads Tech alone is adding 0.421 index points -- more than the S&P's entire 0.39% move -- while Nvidia (NVDA) contributes 0.144 points on its own, 37% of the whole index's move.
Also Moving SpaceX Spectrum Buy Sends Telecom Carriers Tumbling AT&T (-7.16%) and Verizon (-7.01%) are dragging Communication Services to -0.37% (-0.037 pts) even as Alphabet's (GOOGL) 0.58% gain adds back 0.032 points to the same sector.
Worth Knowing Gold Tops $4,200 as Fed Outlook Stays in Focus Not really showing up in equities yet -- gold's 1.27% gain and falling yields both point the same direction (safety-seeking, lower real rates), worth watching if it starts to look less like a hedge and more like a warning.
