Post-Hike Relief Rally Broadens as VIX Craters 12%

Wednesday's quarter-point Fed hike under Chair Kevin Warsh -- unanimous, and delivered despite repeated public pressure from President Trump to cut -- is now reading as a relief event rather than a hawkish surprise: S&P futures are up 1.24% and the VIX has collapsed 12.03% to 15.58 as the uncertainty around the decision itself clears. The rally is broad, not narrow: Nasdaq futures lead at +1.61% on outsized moves in AMD (AMD, $530.67, +3.55% premarket) and Nvidia (NVDA, $218.70, +2.24% premarket), while WTI crude is down 3.00% to $99.36 after Saudi Arabia reportedly offered ship-to-ship crude transfers following a pipeline hit -- easing a separate supply-side worry the same morning. The 10-year yield actually eased slightly to 4.95% even after the hike, and gold's modest 0.59% gain alongside a falling dollar looks more like routine positioning than any flight to safety.
